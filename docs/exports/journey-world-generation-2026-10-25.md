## world-atlas

Use case: illustration-story. Create a new tall portrait game world terrain background, ideally 1:3 aspect ratio. The reference is ONLY a STYLE reference: thin dark hand-drawn outlines, very simple big flat shapes, muted olive, green and brown cartoon palette. The new scene MUST be viewed from HIGH OVERHEAD, nearly top down, like a large explorable landscape. NO sky, NO horizon, NO front-view forest stage. It is a continuous geographical landscape with three biomes seamlessly transitioning vertically: bottom third an olive green woodland with rounded tree canopies, middle third a blue-green lakeshore (lake mostly on the right edge, open grassy banks through the middle), top third a golden birch grove with warm ochre foliage and a few soft rocky hills on the edges. Keep the CENTRAL 45 percent of the entire image mostly open grass, with spacious clearings alternating a little left/right. Trees, lake and rocks frame the outer sides and distinguish biomes. The scene will be horizontally cropped to its middle in a vertical scrolling mobile viewport, so the recognizable biome changes must also be visible near the middle. Do not paint any road: the winding sandy road and interactive camp stops will be drawn as a separate layer. No characters, animals, tents, fires, buildings, text, labels, borders, UI, pins or watermarks. Do not divide this into three panels. One large continuous world, flat cartoon treatment matching the reference, clean big silhouettes, no realistic texture, no 3D.

## camp-lake

Use case: illustration-story. Create a NEW lakeside camping background matching the attached camp background's exact hand-drawn flat cartoon style, thin dark outlines, simple shapes and approximately 1:2 portrait framing. Preserve its functional layout: quiet pale sky in the upper 30%, background tree silhouettes and a blue lake across the upper middle around 30–44%, a broad open grassy camping clearing across the whole lower 60%. Tall rounded trees frame the left and right edges. The lake is visible BEHIND the campsite, never under the character's feet. Reeds and a few stones near the shoreline, very sparse grass tufts on the foreground meadow. Blue-green water, sage trees, olive grass. This is the camp after reaching the lake biome. Same eye-level camera and scale as the reference, no tent, no fire, no person, no dog, no props, no text, no UI. The existing character and objects will be overlaid separately. Calm daytime, minimal shading, no 3D, no painterly texture.
